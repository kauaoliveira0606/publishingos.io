Let's Get You Set Up

⏱ 15 Minutes

Total setup time from start to finish

3 Steps

Join Slack, join Skool, fill out the onboarding form

✅ Done

Your team will follow up within 24 hours

Follow the steps in order. Each step builds on the previous one. Use the same email address for both platforms.

Step 1 of 3

Join Your Private Slack Workspace

Slack is where you communicate directly with your team. Your private channel is already set up and waiting for you. This is your primary line of communication throughout the program.

Step 1.1

Download Slack

If you don't already have Slack installed, download it now. Slack is available for desktop and mobile — we recommend installing both so you never miss a message from your team.

Download Options

Visit slack.com/downloads to get the app for your device.

Desktop App

Available for Mac and Windows. Recommended for daily use.

Mobile App

Available on iOS and Android. Stay connected on the go.

Step 1.2

Join the Workspace & Find Your Channel

Click the invite link below to join the PublishingOS Slack workspace, then locate your private channel in the left sidebar.

Join the Workspace

Click the button to join the PublishingOS Slack workspace. Follow the prompts to create or connect your Slack account.

Join the PublishingOS Slack Workspace

Find Your Private Channel

Once inside the workspace, your private channel appears automatically in the left sidebar. You've been added automatically — no searching needed.

Your channel is named: firstname-lastname-lounge

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Use the same email address you used to register for your sales call. This same email will also be used for Skool.
Step 2 of 3

Join the Community on Skool

Skool is your home base. This is where your course content, resources, community and weekly Q&A calls all live. You need to be inside the community before anything else.

Step 2.1

Accept Your Skool Invite

Check your email inbox for an invitation to join the PublishingOS community on Skool. If you don't see it, check your Spam and Promotions folders.

1. Find the Email

Look for an email from Skool with the subject line referencing PublishingOS. It contains a JOIN NOW button.

2. Create Your Skool Account

Fill in your name, email address, and create a password to complete your Skool account setup and join the group.

3. Start Here

Once inside the community, go to Classroom and open the Start Here module. This is your first step. Do not skip it.

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Use the same email address you used for Slack when joining Skool. Consistency across both platforms is required.
Step 3 of 3

Fill Out Your Onboarding Form

Once inside your private channel, open the Shared Canvas and complete the onboarding form. This gives your team the context they need to start working with you immediately.

Check the Shared Canvas to see the first steps

Your day-by-day plan, resources, and templates — all in one place. Open it first and keep it handy throughout the process.

Fill Out the Form
